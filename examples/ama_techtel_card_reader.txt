Hook, first 3 seconds
"Card reader stops working mid-rush. What do you do first?"

The tip
Start the new year with a plan, so a tech hiccup takes ten minutes instead of the whole afternoon.
First, check the simple stuff. Make sure the card reader is plugged in, the cables are snug, and the screen is on. A loose cord causes more trouble than you'd think.
Second, check one other device. If your phone or office computer can still get online, the problem is probably just the card reader, not your whole connection.
Third, restart only the card reader or register. Turn it off, wait thirty seconds, and turn it back on.
Fourth, have a backup ready. Many payment systems offer an offline mode or a mobile card reader that works from a phone. Set that up now, so you can keep taking payments while you sort things out.
And finally, keep the numbers for your provider and your point-of-sale company taped right by the register, so nobody is hunting for them while customers wait.
