# AMA TechTel — Tech Tip Tuesday: Card Reader Down

- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9`
- **0–3s Visual Hook:** Card reader dies mid-rush — open straight on urgent hands checking snug power & data cords so viewers stop scrolling immediately.
- **Curiosity Loop Strategy:** Tension (dead reader mid-rush) -> Isolation (phone still online, so it's just the terminal) -> Action & Suspense (00:30 power-cycle countdown) -> Relief & Payoff (mobile tap backup + taped support number on register).
- **Visual Consistency Lock:** Warm wooden small-business checkout counter, matte-black countertop EMV card reader beside POS register, warm overhead pendant lights, softly blurred busy shop interior, natural commercial B-roll color grade, 35-50mm f/2.8 shallow depth of field.
- **Gear & Location Notes:** Shoot at one real retail/cafe checkout counter during soft bustle. Lenses: 35mm + 50mm f/2.8. Practical props: matte-black EMV terminal, smartphone with Wi-Fi settings & 00:30 timer, compact wireless tap reader, clear Scotch tape + printed 'SUPPORT / 1-800-555-0199' strip.

## Shot List

### Hook 1 (Top-Left) — 0-3s Visual Hook
- **Script Beat:** Card reader stops working mid-rush. First, check the simple stuff — cables snug, screen on.
- **Visual Instruction:** Up-close of hands checking that power & ethernet cables are plugged snugly into the card reader and tilting terminal to confirm the screen powers on.
- **Framing & Lens:** Extreme Close-Up (50mm f/2.8)
- **Camera Movement:** Handheld tight push-in with brisk, mid-rush energy
- **Lighting & Props:** Matte-black EMV reader, snug black cords, warm counter practicals, blurred customers behind
- **Flux Prompt:** `Photorealistic commercial B-roll still, 50mm f/2.8. Tight close-up of retail employee's hands checking power and ethernet cables snugly into matte-black countertop EMV card reader on warm wood counter, screen lighting up, blurred cafe background.`

### Hook 2 (Top-Right) — Curiosity / Isolation
- **Script Beat:** Second, check one other device. If your phone can still get online, it's just the card reader.
- **Visual Instruction:** Over-the-shoulder of employee checking smartphone showing active Wi-Fi 'Connected' status while the countertop card reader stays dormant and blank beside the POS.
- **Framing & Lens:** Over-the-Shoulder MCU (35mm f/2.8)
- **Camera Movement:** Subtle rack focus from dormant blank card reader to lit phone screen
- **Lighting & Props:** Smartphone displaying Wi-Fi Connected, dormant blank-screen card reader on wood counter
- **Flux Prompt:** `Photorealistic commercial B-roll still, 35mm f/2.8. Over-the-shoulder medium close-up at warm wood checkout counter: employee holds smartphone showing Wi-Fi Connected while dormant matte-black EMV card reader sits blank beside POS.`

### Hook 3 (Bottom-Left) — Action & Wait Loop
- **Script Beat:** Third, restart only the card reader or register. Turn it off, wait thirty seconds, turn it back on.
- **Visual Instruction:** One hand powers down the card reader while the other hand holds a phone/watch in foreground showing a crisp 00:30 countdown timer waiting to reboot.
- **Framing & Lens:** Tight Insert Close-Up (50mm f/2.8)
- **Camera Movement:** Locked-off handheld micro-drift holding both terminal and 00:30 timer sharp
- **Lighting & Props:** Card reader power button, smartphone/watch set to 00:30 countdown timer
- **Flux Prompt:** `Photorealistic commercial B-roll still, 50mm f/2.8. Close-up at warm wood counter: one hand powers down matte-black EMV card reader while other hand holds phone in foreground showing clean 00:30 countdown timer.`

### Hook 4 & 5 (Bottom-Right) — Backup & Payoff CTA
- **Script Beat:** Have a mobile card reader backup ready, and keep provider & POS support numbers taped right by the register.
- **Visual Instruction:** Prepping a compact wireless mobile fallback reader beside smartphone while a printed 'SUPPORT / 1-800-555-0199' strip is taped cleanly onto the POS register bezel.
- **Framing & Lens:** Medium Close-Up (35mm f/2.8)
- **Camera Movement:** Smooth lateral slide from mobile backup reader across to taped support label
- **Lighting & Props:** Pocket wireless tap reader, smartphone, clear-taped 'SUPPORT / 1-800-555-0199' strip on POS
- **Flux Prompt:** `Photorealistic commercial B-roll still, 35mm f/2.8. Medium close-up at warm wood counter: employee sets compact white mobile tap card reader next to phone, while 'SUPPORT / 1-800-555-0199' is taped cleanly onto POS register bezel.`
