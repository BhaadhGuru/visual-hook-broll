PKG: NORTHSIDE WATER MAIN REPAIR

VO:
Crews spent early Tuesday morning blocking off two lanes along Oak Ridge Boulevard after a 12-inch water main ruptured beneath the intersection. Water pooled along the curb as public works trucks arrived with barricades and orange traffic cones.

SOT (MARIA LOPEZ, HOMEOWNER):
"I turned on the kitchen faucet at six in the morning and nothing came out. Then I walked outside and saw water running down the sidewalk past our mailbox."

VO:
Utility workers used a backhoe to clear wet asphalt from the break while a technician shut the main valve near the corner hydrant. City officials say water pressure should return to normal by five o'clock this evening.

STANDUP (REPORTER):
Public works crews expect to repave this stretch of Oak Ridge Boulevard by Friday, and drivers are asked to detour along Cedar Avenue until the cones come down.
